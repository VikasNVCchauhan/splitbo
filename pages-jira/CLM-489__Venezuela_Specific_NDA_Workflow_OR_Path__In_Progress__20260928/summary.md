## Description

*User Story:*

*As a* LATAM territory sales user

*So that* I can efficiently process NDAs for Venezuela prospective customers while ensuring proper compliance review

*I want to* initiate an Ironclad NDA workflow specifically designed for Venezuela accounts that includes mandatory compliance certification and structured approval processes

*Acceptance Criteria:*

*Eligibility & Targeting:*
 * [ ] Workflow is only available for accounts with Venezuela billing and shipping addresses
 * [ ] Limited to eligible accounts as defined by business rules
 * [ ] Accessible to LATAM territory GEO sales users

*Document Configuration:*
 * [ ] Uses "Venezuela Prospective Customer Due Diligence NDA" document template
 * [ ] Contains customized due diligence language for Venezuela requirements
 * [ ] Document language is English
 * [ ] Includes specific property and contract layout sections

*Workflow Process:*
 * [ ] Pre-signed document with mandatory approval layer before execution
 * [ ] Built-in approval workflow (not ad-hoc approver selection)

*Approval Chain:*
 * [ ] Stewart's team (compliance) review integrated into workflow before customer signing of the pre-signed document
 * [ ] Sequential approval process ensures compliance review occurs before final signature

*Signing Authority:*
 * [ ] Liz Kelly designated as primary signer
 * [ ] Any director level or above can serve as alternate signer
 * [ ] Proper signature authority validation

*Definition of Done:*
 * Venezuela-specific NDA workflow is configured in Ironclad
 * Compliance certification prerequisite is enforced
 * Approval chain functions correctly with proper routing
 * Document generates with correct template and customizations
 * Testing completed with compliance team
